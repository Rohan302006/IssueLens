How IssueLens Resolves Campus Problems

Designed specifically for university students and facilities teams, eliminating bureaucracy with a direct 3-step reporting workflow.

1

Quick EN Login

No complex passwords to remember or reset. Enter your university-assigned Enrollment Number (EN) to instantly access your personal dashboard.

2

Submit with Photo Evidence

Select the issue category, describe the problem and location, and attach photographic proof directly from mobile camera or desktop.

3

Live Resolution Tracking

Follow the live status badge as your complaint moves from Pending to In Progress to Resolved with full administrative accountability.

Campus Coverage

What Can You Report?

IssueLens handles facilities and maintenance categories across classrooms, labs, hostels, and common areas.

⚡
Electricity & Power
Outages, damaged switchboards, broken corridor lights, and AC failures.
💧
Water & Plumbing
Water cooler faults, pipe leakages, low water pressure, and restroom plumbing.
🧹
Hygiene & Cleanliness
Waste accumulation, washroom sanitization, and classroom cleanliness.
📶
Campus WiFi & Network
Access point disconnections, hostel router dead zones, and lab network drops.
🏫
Classroom Infrastructure
Damaged benches, broken audio/projector setups, fans, and podium fixtures.
🏠
Hostels & Living Facilities
Hostel room fixtures, geyser repairs, door locks, and mess facility complaints.
